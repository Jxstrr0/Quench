| 0.11.1.0 | PR 11b: Clay and the hardening line | **Clay on the back of the blade.** A new **Clay** button at the grinder steps through thin, half, deep and none. Bring the clayed blade to heat and quench it: under the clay the steel stays soft, the bare edge goes hard. The edge is as hard as ever, the blade bends further in the vice, and a water quench is much safer (W2 in water: about 16 cracks in 20 bare, about 3 in 20 with clay). **Etch it and the hardening line shows**, in the forge and as a wavy line on the blade card. A blow or the belt knocks the clay off; the quench washes it off; a new hardening heat wipes the soft back. A sword with a soft back bends and stays bent at the flex test, and the report says why. Old saves and kept blades still load. 11 new tests (365 in total). |
